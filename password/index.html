Password

Two teams of two. Clue-givers take turns giving one-word clues; the sooner your partner guesses, the more the word is worth.

How to play

  1. Two teams, each with a clue-giver and a guesser. Both clue-givers see the secret word; the guessers don't.
  2. The team shown as guessing goes: its clue-giver says one word as a clue, and its guesser gets one guess.
  3. Right? That team scores what the word is worth. Wrong? The word loses a point and the other team gets the next clue.
  4. Teams take turns giving the first clue on each new word. After 10 clues, nobody scores it.
  5. Classic scoring: each word starts at 10 points and loses one with every clue. First team to the target score wins.
  6. Simple scoring: every right answer is 1 point. Play a set number of rounds; in each round both teams start one word. Most points wins.

No gestures, no rhymes, no "sounds like", and no part of the word.

One phone is enough: the clue-givers share the Moderator screen. Add a Scoreboard on a TV or tablet facing the guessers.
