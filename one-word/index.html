Just One Word

One player guesses a secret word. Everyone else writes a one-word clue on their own phone, all at once. Matching clues cancel out, so be original.

How to play

  1. Put the Host screen on a TV (or a tablet everyone can see). Players scan its QR code with their phones and type their names.
  2. Each word, one player is the guesser. Their phone doesn't show the word. Everyone else on their team sees it and writes one word as a clue, without talking.
  3. Matching clues cancel out: the same word, or the same root, like SNOW and SNOWY or RUN and RUNNING. A clue that's the secret word itself is out too. The phones mark these; the clue-givers can tap a clue to cancel or keep it, then tap Show the clues.
  4. The guesser sees the clues that are left and gets one guess, out loud. A clue-giver taps Right or Wrong; the guesser can Pass instead.
  5. Together: everyone plays as one team through a pile of words. Right scores one; a pass loses that word; a wrong guess loses that word and the next one. How many can you get?
  6. Two teams: the teams take turns, one word each. Only the playing team writes clues; the other team watches. Most right answers wins.

Teams need at least 4 players (2 a team), and play best with 6 or more. Together, 3 or more.
