Watch Ya Mouth

The reader wears a cheek retractor and reads each phrase out loud; their team has to work out what they're saying. One device, held by the reader.

How to play

  1. Each team picks a reader, who puts in the cheek retractor and holds this phone.
  2. The reader taps Start round and reads each phrase out loud — no gestures, no spelling.
  3. When the team says the phrase, tap Got It for a point. Stuck? Tap Skip.
  4. When the clock runs out, pass the phone (and a clean mouthpiece) to the next team.

Keep the screen turned away from the guessers.
