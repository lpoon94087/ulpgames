Word Generator

Words for Charades, Pictionary, Password and any other guessing game. Play on one device, or add viewer screens that show each word.

How to play

Just words — tick the lists you want and tap Next word whenever you need one. Words don't repeat until the list runs out.

Timed rounds — teams take turns. The player up holds the phone, gets their team to guess as many words as they can before the clock runs out, and taps Got It or Skip.

  • Charades — act it out, no talking.
  • Pictionary — draw it, no letters or numbers.
  • Describe it — any list works: talk around the word without saying any part of it.

More screens — the moderator has every control. Other devices choose Viewer and join with the moderator's room code or QR code. They show the current word, the clock and the scores, but can't change anything. A Scoreboard joins the same way and shows only the clock and scores, so the guessing team can watch it; after each round it lists the words got and missed.

Playing on one phone? Just pick Moderator. For more screens, open this page on them, choose Viewer or Scoreboard and type the room code.
